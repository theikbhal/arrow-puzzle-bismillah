Matching sounds (synthesized, no audio files)

Each cleared arrow plays a note one step higher on a gentle scale, so clearing a level sounds like a melody.
A blocked tap gives a soft low thud, and there are separate sounds for winning, losing and hints.
Settings lets you pick the sound style: Chime, Wood (tasbih-bead click) or Water drop, and set the volume.

Background sounds

Built in, no files needed: Rain, Brown noise, Fireplace, Breath, Nature and Calm pad. Music is off by default and has its own volume slider.
Islamic audio: I can't supply or verify Quran recitation or nasheeds, so I built a place for your own files. Put a recording in audio/, add one line in audio/tracks.js, and it shows up in Settings → Music, grouped as quran, nasheed or dhikr. audio/README.md lists sources to check, such as quran.com, everyayah.com and quranicaudio.com. Read each site's terms before using audio in an app, and check the reciter yourself.
I kept recitation off by default and separate from the game sound effects. If you're unsure about recitation as game background, ask your imam; vocals-only nasheed or dhikr are the usual safer choice.

Languages (27)

Indian (13): Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Urdu and Nepali.
World (13): English, Spanish, French, German, Dutch, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Indonesian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
Arabic and Urdu switch the page to right-to-left.
The other Indian languages (Konkani, Maithili, Kashmiri, Sindhi, Bodo, Dogri, Santali, Manipuri and Sanskrit) are not translated yet. They fall back to English until someone adds a line in i18n.js.
I wrote these translations myself, and they cover only the main screens. Please have a native speaker check them, especially Telugu, Tamil and the other Indian languages. Theme names and some settings labels stay in English.

300 levels

Levels 1–2 are handmade and levels 3–12 are sized to your photos. After that, boards grow from about 10×12 to 15×21 and arrows from 16 to 57, with longer arrows on later levels.
Levels 1–5 are Normal, 6–120 are Medium and 121–300 are Hard.
Every level is checked as solvable.
The title now shows only the level number, with dots for difficulty, so it needs no translation.
To regenerate or change the levels, run node tools/build_levels.js 300 inside level_creator, then copy the new levels.js into the player and renderer folders.

Download the all zip, or just the player zip. Your old browser progress may show stale stars, so clear the site data if the level grid looks odd.
